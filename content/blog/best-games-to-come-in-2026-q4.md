# Best Games to Come in 2026 Q4

The final quarter of 2026 is shaping up to be one of the most densely packed periods for video games in recent memory. With blockbuster sequels, beloved franchises returning, and several genre-defining releases all landing between October and December, Q4 offers something for nearly every type of player. Whether you're a fan of open-world crime epics, tactical combat, racing, or rhythm games, the next three months deliver an embarrassment of riches.

## October 2026 – A Flood of Franchise Revivals

October kicks off Q4 with remarkable intensity. The month begins with back-to-back releases on October 1, led by [Rayman Legends Retold](https://thebesties.substack.com/p/2026s-best-games-and-whats-still) for PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch 2, and PC. The same day brings [End of Abyss](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q45uccMx_J0), a survival-horror title running on Unreal Engine 5 for PS5 and PC, suggesting players won't have much time to catch their breath.

October 2 follows with [Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve](https://thebesties.substack.com/p/2026s-best-games-and-whats-still) on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC, continuing the long-running aerial combat series. Just days later, October 6 sees two major releases: [Star Wars: Galactic Racer](https://thebesties.substack.com/p/2026s-best-games-and-whats-still) on PS5, Xbox Series X|S, and PC, alongside [Gears of War Eday](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEnINYWIHQQ) on Xbox Series and PC. The Gears entry is a prequel set 14 years before the original, depicting Emergence Day when the Locust Horde first emerged.

Mid-month October delivers a strong slate. [Planet 2](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEnINYWIHQQ) arrives on October 13 for PC, PS5, and Xbox Series, bringing enhanced zoo management mechanics, wildlife reserves, and deeper staff systems to the beloved park simulation franchise. On October 15, [Castlevania: Belmont's Curse](https://thebesties.substack.com/p/2026s-best-games-and-whats-still) launches across PS5, Xbox Series X|S, Switch, and PC, reviving one of gaming's most iconic horror-platformer franchises.

The month closes with two Nintendo-focused releases. [Nintendo Switch Sports Resort](https://thebesties.substack.com/p/2026s-best-games-and-whats-still) arrives on October 22 exclusively for Switch 2, expanding the casual sports formula. The month's most anticipated October release comes on October 23 with [Final Fantasy Resonance](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q45uccMx_J0), built on Unreal Engine 5 for PS5, Xbox, PC, and Switch 2.

Rounding out October, [Valor Mortise](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEnINYWIHQQ) enters the market as a Napoleonic-era souls-like title, alongside [Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qEnINYWIHQQ), which returns the franchise to a North Korea setting with Infinity Ward at the helm.

## November 2026 – Grand Theft Auto 6 Dominates

November belongs to one of gaming's most anticipated releases. [Grand Theft Auto 6](https://thebesties.substack.com/p/2026s-best-games-and-whats-still) officially launches on November 19 for PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, marking Rockstar's first new entry in the franchise since 2013. The game returns players to Vice City, concluding one of the longest development cycles in gaming history.

The middle of the month brings smaller-scale releases. [Where the Seeds Fall](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/new-pc-games-2026) and [Edge of Memories](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/new-pc-games-2026) both release on November 5 via Steam. The former offers 3D platforming from a new Cygames studio, while the latter presents a JRPG-inspired adventure.

## December 2026 – Strategy and Simulation Titles Close the Year

December shifts toward strategy and simulation, with [Warhammer 40,000: Dawn of War 4](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/new-pc-games-2026) leading the charge as the next entry in the acclaimed real-time strategy series. The month also sees releases across multiple genres for players who have exhausted their November blockbusters.

Beyond the specific monthly titles, several games are slated for Q4 without firm release dates. The Wikipedia-sourced [Dog Man: Bark in Action](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_video_games_released_in_2026) lands on Windows, Nintendo Switch, PlayStation, and Xbox as an adventure-platformer from Floor 84 Studio. [Drunken Goddess Reflux](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_video_games_released_in_2026) brings simulation gameplay to PC, while [Unpetrified: Echoes of Nature](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_video_games_released_in_2026) serves as a shoot-em-up port for Switch, PS5, and Xbox Series X|S.

Horror fans have options including [The Alighieri Circle: Dante's Bloodline](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_video_games_released_in_2026), a psychological horror title for Switch 2, and [Animal Use Protocol](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_video_games_released_in_2026), a survival horror game for PC from The Brotherhood. [Dreadmoor](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q45uccMx_J0), another Unreal Engine 5 title, targets a Q4 PC release.

Several major Q4 releases also appear on broader calendars without specific dates. These include [Broken Sword: Parzival's Stone](https://www.releases.com/calendar/games/2026-q4-est), [Hytale](https://www.releases.com/calendar/games/2026-q4-est), [Cronos: Lazarus](https://www.releases.com/calendar/games/2026-q4-est), [Thief: The Dark Project Remastered](https://www.releases.com/calendar/games/2026-q4-est), and [Bestiario](https://www.releases.com/calendar/games/2026-q4-est), each representing different genres from adventure to action RPGs.

## What Makes This Q4 Unusually Strong

The density of Q4 2026 is notable not just for individual titles, but for the diversity of experiences on offer. Players can transition from the high-speed aerial combat of Ace Combat 8 to the strategic depth of Warhammer 40K: Dawn of War 4, or from the nostalgic platforming of Rayman Legends Retold to the cutting-edge graphical fidelity of Final Fantasy Resonance.

Multiple engines power these releases, with Unreal Engine 5 appearing across several major titles including Final Fantasy Resonance, Dreadmoor, End of Abyss, and Mortal Shell 2. This technological foundation suggests that visual quality will be a common thread throughout the quarter's biggest releases.

The console landscape is also well-represented. While Grand Theft Auto 6 remains a PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S exclusive at launch, other major titles like Final Fantasy Resonance and Rayman Legends Retold extend to Switch 2 and PC. Meanwhile, Nintendo's Switch Sports Resort anchors the platform holder's Q4 offering.

## A Quarter Not to Miss

With over two dozen confirmed or strongly anticipated releases spanning every major genre, Q4 2026 presents an opportunity for players to dive into everything from intimate indie adventures to the next evolution of gaming's biggest franchises. The question isn't whether there will be standout titles this fall and winter, but how many of them will achieve classic status.

For players planning their holiday gaming sessions, October through December offer a rare combination of blockbuster events and hidden gems, all supported by next-generation technology and development teams that have had years to refine their craft. The back half of 2026 isn't just dense—it's a masterclass in release scheduling, giving each major title room to breathe while ensuring there's always something new on the horizon.
