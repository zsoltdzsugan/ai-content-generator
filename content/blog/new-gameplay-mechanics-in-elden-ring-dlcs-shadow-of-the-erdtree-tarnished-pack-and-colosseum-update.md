# New Gameplay Mechanics in Elden Ring DLCs: Shadow of the Erdtree, Tarnished Pack, and Colosseum Update

## Shadow of the Erdtree Expansion Mechanics

The Shadow of the Erdtree expansion, released in June 2024, introduces several new gameplay mechanics that expand upon Elden Ring's core systems. According to the PlayStation Store page, the DLC adds "new weapons, equipment, weapon skills and magic not found in the base game," directly expanding the player's combat options beyond what was available in the original 2022 release.

Specific weapon types highlighted in promotional materials include a dueling shield, backhand blades, and a new great katana. A YouTube content creator noted these appeared as "base versions of some of the new weapon types in the DLC" accessible before fully entering the expansion content, suggesting early-game availability for these mechanics. The IGN review corroborates the addition of new combat tools, listing "cool new weapons, spells, Ashes of War, Spirit Ashes, talismans, and more" as key elements that provide "novel ways to tackle its memorably brutal" encounters.

Beyond weapons, the expansion introduces entirely new skill systems. The PlayStation page explicitly states Shadow of the Erdtree adds "weapon skills and magic not found in the base game," indicating new Ashes of War (which modify weapon attacks) and spell varieties. The IGN review further details that players can experiment with these new systems to "find even more novel ways to tackle" challenges, emphasizing how these mechanics integrate with Elden Ring's signature build diversity. The expansion also features new Spirit Ashes (summonable allies) and talismans (passive equipment items), expanding strategic options for both offense and defense.

These mechanical additions are designed to function within the existing Elden Ring framework rather than overhaul it. As noted by multiple reviewers, the DLC "condenses [the base game's] landmark RPG experience into a relatively compact 20-25 hour campaign" while preserving the core loop of exploration, experimentation, and overcoming difficult encounters through player-driven solutions.

## Tarnished Pack Mechanics

Released alongside the Elden Ring Tarnished Edition for Nintendo Switch 2 in August 2026, the Tarnished Pack DLC focuses on mechanical additions usable in both the base game and Shadow of the Erdtree expansion. Bandai Namco's official description confirms it grants "2 new starting classes, new armor, and a new Torrent appearance customization feature."

The two new starting classes represent a fundamental shift in how players begin their journey, offering different initial stat distributions, equipment loadouts, and role-playing foundations compared to the original ten classes. This affects early-game progression and build diversity from the very first moments of play.

New armor sets provide fresh aesthetic and statistical options for character customization. While specific defensive values or set bonuses aren't detailed in the sources, the addition of armor expands the equipment progression system, allowing players to tailor their survivability and appearance further.

The Torrent appearance customization feature adds three unique visual variants for the Spectral Steed mount. A YouTube video transcript describes these as "three new costumes for Torrent," with the first example shown in the trailer. Importantly, this customization is purely cosmetic—sources do not indicate any changes to mounted combat mechanics, speed, or summoning behavior associated with these visual options. The feature is explicitly noted as usable across both the base game and Shadow of the Erdtree expansion, ensuring broad accessibility.

## Colosseum Update Mechanics

Predating both major DLCs, the free Colosseum update released in December 2022 introduced new multiplayer mechanics to Elden Ring. According to the DLC wiki page, this update added "Colosseum battles, where players can engage in new PvP modes and six-player multiplayer."

This represented a significant expansion of Elden Ring's online capabilities beyond the base game's invasion and summoning systems. The introduction of structured PvP modes within the Colosseum venue provided dedicated spaces for player-versus-player combat with specific rulesets, distinct from the open-world invasions of the base game. The six-player multiplayer capacity also marked a substantial increase from the previous limit, enabling larger-scale cooperative or competitive sessions within these designated areas.

While the wiki does not detail specific PvP mode rules (such as team sizes, objectives, or victory conditions), it confirms the update's focus on creating new frameworks for player interaction. This update laid groundwork for future online features by demonstrating FromSoftware's willingness to expand Elden Ring's multiplayer scope through free post-launch content.

## Synthesis of Mechanical Expansion

Across all DLCs, FromSoftware has consistently expanded Elden Ring's mechanics through additive rather than transformative approaches. The Shadow of the Erdtree expansion deepens combat complexity with new weapon classes, skills, and magic systems. The Tarnished Pack enhances character creation and customization options through new starting classes and cosmetic mount variations. The Colosseum update broadens the multiplayer landscape with structured PvP and larger session sizes.

Critically, sources emphasize that these additions maintain fidelity to Elden Ring's core design philosophy. The IGN review notes Shadow of the Erdtree "raises the bar for single-player DLC expansions" by taking "everything that made the base game such a landmark RPG" and refining it into a "tight package." Similarly, the Tarnished Pack's content is described as usable across both game states, ensuring mechanical cohesion rather than fragmentation. This approach allows players to engage with new systems while retaining the fundamental loop of exploration, build experimentation, and overcoming challenge that defines the Elden Ring experience. The mechanical expansions serve to enrich—not alter—the foundation established by the base game.
