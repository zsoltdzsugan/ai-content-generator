# Top 10 Games of 2026

## The Landscape of 2026 Gaming

The first half of 2026 has been marked by a mix of long‑awaited sequels, revivals of classic franchises, and a steady stream of high‑quality indie titles.  The industry is holding its breath for the November launch of Grand Theft Auto VI, but titles such as **Resident Evil: Requiem**, **Crimson Desert**, and early‑access follow‑ups like **Subnautica 2** and **Slay the Spire 2** have already generated strong buzz.  At the same time, major publishers are facing challenges: Xbox has laid off thousands of staff, Ubisoft closed studios and delayed several projects, and smaller teams such as those behind **MIO: Memories in Orbit** have shuttered after disappointing sales.  Despite these pressures, the year’s release calendar remains crowded, with a variety of genres—from hard‑hitting shooters to narrative‑driven RPGs—catering to diverse player tastes.

## The Top 10 Games of 2026

### 1. Resident Evil: Requiem  
Regarded as the final chapter of the long‑running Resident Evil saga, *Resident Evil: Requiem* brings Leon Kennedy and newcomer Grace Ashcroft back to Raccoon City.  The game holds a 9.2 rating on IMDb’s 2026 top‑10 list and scores 89 on OpenCritic, reflecting strong critical and community interest.  NPR highlighted its “snappy, scary, breath‑taking” pacing, while DayOne praised its balance of survival horror and “balls‑to‑the‑wall action.”  The title’s February release has made it a focal point for both fans and critics alike.

### 2. Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter  
*Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter* continues the beloved *Trails* saga, following Estelle Bright as she searches for the truth behind Joshua’s disappearance.  It earned a 92 Metascore on Metacritic and a 91 rating on OpenCritic, positioning it as one of the year’s most acclaimed RPGs.  The game topped the OpenCritic “Best Games of 2026” list, and NPR noted its “must‑play” status among fans of narrative‑driven JRPGs.

### 3. DOOM: The Dark Ages – Revelations  
The *DOOM* franchise returns with *The Dark Ages – Revelations*, an expansion that adds new levels, puzzles, demons, and the deadly Chain Spear.  It holds a 91 Metascore on Metacritic and an 89 rating on OpenCritic.  NPR described it as a “brutal new chapter” that “rewards mastery with a potent combination of power and mobility,” while DayOne highlighted its deeper puzzles and new combat dimensions.

### 4. Forza Horizon 6  
*Forza Horizon 6* expands the open‑world racing experience with a fresh map and updated graphics.  It received a 91 Metascore on Metacritic and a 91 rating on OpenCritic, and appears on the OpenCritic top‑10 list.  NPR mentioned it alongside other early‑year releases, and the game’s May launch made it a standout for racing enthusiasts.

### 5. 007 First Light  
*007 First Light* is the latest entry in the iconic spy franchise, holding an 8.7 rating on IMDb’s 2026 top‑10 list.  NPR noted its place among the “first half of the year” crowd, emphasizing its polished stealth‑action gameplay and cinematic presentation.

### 6. Assassin’s Creed: Black Flag Resynced  
*Assassin’s Creed: Black Flag Resynced* revives the pirate‑themed classic with updated visuals and mechanics.  It appears third on IMDb’s 2026 top‑10 ranking, and DayOne highlighted its “tight platforming” and “satisfying combat,” marking it as a notable entry for both series veterans and newcomers.

### 7. Mina the Hollower  
*Mina the Hollower* is a story‑rich, turn‑based RPG that blends social anxiety themes with a corporate conspiracy.  It earned an 87 Metascore on Metacritic and a 91 OpenCritic rating, and NPR listed it among its favorite titles of the year.  The game’s unique tone and strategic depth have drawn praise from both critics and players.

### 8. Crimson Desert  
*Crimson Desert* dominated the early months of 2026, according to NPR, and holds the second spot on the TopWarzoneMoments user‑score list.  While specific scores are not widely reported, its prominence in multiple media outlets underscores its impact on the gaming conversation.

### 9. Pokémon Pokopia  
*Pokémon Pokopia* entered the scene in March and secured a 89 rating on OpenCritic, placing it tenth on the OpenCritic “Best Games of 2026” list.  NPR referenced it as a notable spin‑off, and the game’s March release has already attracted a dedicated fanbase.

### 10. Sektori  
*Sektori* is an intense, fast‑paced twin‑stick shooter that combines hard‑hitting techno music with high‑adrenaline gameplay.  It boasts a 94 Metascore on Metacritic, the highest among the titles covered, and was highlighted in the Metacritic “Best Games This Year” roundup.  Though it appears only in the Metacritic list, its strong score and unique audio‑visual style make it a standout indie title of 2026.

## Conclusion

2026 offers a diverse portfolio of games that cater to a wide range of preferences, from the horror‑filled return of *Resident Evil: Requiem* to the narrative depth of *Trails in the Sky 2nd Chapter* and the high‑octane action of *Sektori*.  The year’s landscape reflects both the resilience of established franchises and the continued vitality of indie innovations.  As the release calendar progresses toward the highly anticipated *Grand Theft Auto VI* in November, the titles listed above represent the most notable experiences that have already shaped the gaming conversation in 2026.
